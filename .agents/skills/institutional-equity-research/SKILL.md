# Institutional Stock Analysis System Skill

## Objective
Execute institutional-grade fundamental equity research following the 4-step framework:
1. **Longitudinal Narrative & Execution Audit**: Track 8–12 quarters of guidance vs execution, goalpost shifts, and dropped KPIs.
2. **Forensic Footnote & SEC Filing Extraction**: Scrutinize 10-K Section 1A and Section 7, off-balance-sheet commitments, customer/supplier concentration (>10%), and accounting policy/accrual changes.
3. **Morningstar Economic Moat Simulation**: Evaluate the 4 moat pillars (Switching Costs, Network Effects, Cost Advantages, Intangible Assets), assign rating (Wide/Narrow/None), and simulate a Bull vs. Bear debate on the primary existential threat.
4. **Unit Economics & Operating Leverage Audit**: 3–5 year Gross Margin, Operating Margin, FCF conversion trends, SG&A leverage test, and ROIC vs WACC spread.
